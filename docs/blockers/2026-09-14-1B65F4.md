## Why the recipe does not fit

Every recovered spawn child this stint reuses one of the four proven
`spawn_region` allocator arms unchanged. `1B52A0` is not among them, so
recovering `1B65F4` needs either a fifth `_SPAWN_REGION_ARMS` row (its own
pool base, count, direction, exhaustion address and per-slot cost facts,
proven the way `scripts/spawn_allocator_facts.py` already proves the
other four) or confirmation that `1B52A0` is a thin wrapper around one of
the four existing arms reached through an extra indirection not yet
traced. Neither was attempted this stint; the entry was set aside in
favor of the remaining clone-shaped targets with a much higher
value-to-risk ratio.

## Supervisor resolution (15 September)

`1B52A0` is not a fifth allocator.  Traced from
`artifacts/evidence/spawn/1B65F4-kind00-p0.state` (`facts --park 1B52A0`):
49 instructions, 746 cycles, RAM-only, calling the already-proven
`1AE262` free-slot selector over the `FF7F06` pool (20 slots of 0x42) and
then the already-proven initializer `1AE30A` (`initialize_object`), then
storing the template `1B7FE4` fields into the found slot.  Recover
`1B65F4` as a composition of those two adapters plus its own guard
(`TST.B FFF10E`) and selector (`CMPI.B #5, FF7E26`), the way the
`1AE9E0` and `1AE9A8` leaves composed `_clear_objects` with a
re-template.  Sweep `FF7E26` and `FFF10E` with `--vary` for the arms the
recording never took; the pool-exhausted arm is the `1AE262` scan
running out (`Z` set at `1AE278`).
