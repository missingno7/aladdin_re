## Where

- `0030CC`'s own per-object body, `003186`-`0032BE`, called from the
  200-entry object-table scan (`00313A`-`00315C`) for every live
  `BOX_SCAN_TABLE` entry, per `docs/gods/blockers/2026-09-19-0030CC.md`'s
  own assignment.
- `003284` onward (`003284`-`003BF0`+), reached from `003186`'s own tail
  (`0031DE bra.w $3284`, `00325A`, `003248`) with the entry's `x`/`y` in
  `d0`/`d1` and the entry's own achievements-record `+4` status word (a
  `RECORD_TABLE` index, the SAME table `004926`/`0048EA`/`004A0A` already
  read) in `d2` -- disassembled fresh this session; the whole region

## Code

`NEW_GODS_SUBSYSTEM`.  `003186`'s own head (phase byte read, the
`0033D4`/`0033EC` per-phase delta/cue tables, the phase clamp at `0x25`)
matches the assignment's own read exactly and is tractable on its own.
But its own tail (`0031DE`/`00325A`/`003248`, every live entry whose phase
reached the terminal `0x18` or whose position moved) unconditionally
falls into `003284`, which is not a small dispatcher: it is itself a
`RECORD_TABLE`-status-keyed (`d2`) chain of real, materially different
sub-dispatchers, each with its own real device or cross-subsystem work:

1. **`003284`-`0032C2`**: reads the achievements-record status word
   (`+4(a1)`, the SAME `achievements.RECORD_TABLE` arithmetic) and, for
   status `<3`, `==3`, and `==4`, branches to three further chains
   (`0x36E2`, `0x39EA`, `0x3AE6`) via `bgt`/`beq`/fallthrough; status `>=
   4`/`3` compose `0x39EA`/`0x36E2`; the `<3` arm falls through a
   secondary counter (`FFFFF260`, capped at `0x14`) that buffers
   `(x,y,status)` triples into a growing list at `FFFFF262` (a THIRD
   record type, not `BOX_SCAN_TABLE` or `TRIGGER_TABLE`) before reaching
   `0x32F6`'s own SECOND dispatch, this one over `(a1)` (the record's own
   FIRST word, not its status) against literal kind constants `0x53`,
   `0x36`, `0x6D`, `0x6E`, a `0x40`-`0x43` range, each of which calls
   `003480` (a screen-visibility gate returning through `rtr`, a NEW
   return convention this module has not modelled) then `bsr.w $18C8`
   (the ALREADY-RECOVERED sprite emitter, `sprite_emit_plan` -- real,
   reachable device work) or falls to a THIRD dispatch at `0x330C`/
   `0x3330` reading a WORD from `FFFFF206` (an already-named animation
   counter, `tick-map.md`'s own phase counters) as an index into a
   further jump table (`0x334A`, `0x336C`) of MORE such
   `003480`/`0018C8` pairs.
2. **`0x36E2`-`0x3BF0`**: three more `(a1)` dispatches (status `<3`'s own
   companions), one of which (`0x36E2`) is a genuine **indirect `jsr`
   through a real function-pointer table** at ROM `0x370E` -- 13 entries,
   each `(kind: word, handler: long)`, most handlers pointing at
   `0018C8` directly but several at distinct, unexamined ROM functions
   (`0x377A`, `0x37A0`, `0x375C`, `0x37C6`, `0x3884`, `0x3896`), each of
   which itself calls a DIFFERENT native-ish routine, `0x1810` (NOT
   `0018C8`; touches the solid/object definition table at `0x66794`,
   `game.solids`'s own territory, and the animation table at `0x19D2`
   `game.animation` already partly names) with its own further indexed
   dispatch over `FFFFF258` (one of `0030CC`'s own phase counters).
   `0x3AE6`'s own chain reads a SECOND persistent record at `FFFFF24C`
   (a fourth structure) and compares it against the entry's own `x`/`y`.
3. **A pickup-award integration**: `003620`'s own arm (`RECORD_TABLE`
   status `>= 0xC0`) calls `jsr $12C80` directly, which itself compares
   the record's own kind against `pickups.GROUP_TABLES` (`FFFFEF8C`,
   `FFFFF01E`, `FFFFF0B0` -- the SAME three words already named in
   `game/pickups.py`) and, on a miss, chains through THREE more real
   calls (`0x12D30`, `0x12A3E`, `0x12A34`, `0x12A9E`x2, `0x11468`) that
   read `pickups.ITEM_RECORDS` (ROM `0x12D04`, confirmed byte-identical
   to the constant `game/pickups.py` already names) and a further
   per-record table at `FFFFF552` this module has not named.
4. **`003BBA`-`003BF0`**: a fourth `FFFFF260`-gated buffer-fill
   (mirroring #1's own shape) ending in yet another `003480`/`0018C8`
   pair, reached when the achievements-record status is `>= 0xC0`
   (post-adjustment) via `subi.w #$c0,d2; addi.w #$84,d2`.

None of this is one bounded dispatcher over a small witnessed domain: it
is a real, multi-hundred-instruction network of per-status and per-kind
sub-dispatchers sharing four distinct persistent structures (the
`achievements.RECORD_TABLE` status word, the `FFFFF262` triple-buffer
gated by `FFFFF260`, the `FFFFF24C` second record, and `pickups`' own
group/item tables), two of which (`003480`'s own `rtr`-based screen gate,
and the `0370E` function-pointer table) are execution shapes this
project has not yet named, and at least one real device operation
(`0018C8`, already recovered) reached through at least three independent
paths.

## Why the current mechanism does not fit

This is not a bounded leaf, nor a small recipe-6a family over one
witnessed selector: it is a genuine second subsystem (comparable in
scope to the whole aim/creature-targeting family this grinder took
roughly a dozen sessions to close) with at least four distinct persistent
structures, an unmodelled return convention (`003480`'s own `rtr`), a
real indirect-`jsr` function-pointer table (a shape no game in this
repository has proven -- `NEW_SHARED_MECHANISM` territory if the table
itself needs a general mechanism, though each individual handler may
turn out to be an ordinary leaf once censused), and cross-cutting reads
of `pickups`' own already-named tables from a THIRD call site. The
`0030CC` assignment's own text anticipated exactly this ("`003284` (not
read here)"); this doc is the promised read.

## Decision (supervisor, 19 September)

This is the **object draw-and-update** subsystem, and every piece named
above is a shape the project has already recovered once: `003480` is a
screen-visibility gate returning through `rtr` (the convention
`00B8C2` established); `001810` is a second sprite emitter with a
screen-margin test in front of a device upload — `0018C8`'s recipe (a
seam), with `game.solids`' definition table and `game.animation`'s remap
as its inputs; the six distinct handlers of the `00370E` table are
callers of `001810` (seams over a seam, `004790`'s shape); the table
dispatch at `0036E2` is a recipe-6a family over the record's kind word;
the `FFF262` buffer under `FFF260` is a list append (a pool shape); the
`FFF24C` record is a named field; `012C80` is a chain over
`pickups.GROUP_TABLES`/`ITEM_RECORDS`, already named; the status chains
are families over the status word.  A census at the body's entry
explodes because it spans all of them — the state machine had the same
shape and was censused per state.  Census each piece at its own entry.

Order, bottom-up: `003480`; `001810` (seam); the six `00370E` handlers
(`00377A`, `0037A0`, `00375C`, `0037C6`, `003884`, `003896`) and the
`0036E2` dispatch as the family over them and `0018C8`; the `FFF262`
append; `012C80` and its chain (`012D30`, `012A3E`, `012A34`, `012A9E`,
`011468`) with `pickups`' tables; the status chains (`0032F6` and the
`FFF206`-indexed tables, `0039EA`, `003AE6`, `003BBA`) as families;
`003186`'s head; `003284` composing the chains; `0030CC` as the scan with
its card (the sound requests as pending effects).  `game/world.py` names
the record, the buffers and the phase tables.  Only a census showing an
unbounded loop or an unmodelled device makes a blocker package.
