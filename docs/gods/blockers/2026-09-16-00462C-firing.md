## Why the current mechanism does not fit

This is not the single bounded "kind selects one of N self-contained
bodies" shape recipe 6a (and `00470C`'s own recovery) covers. It nests
three different unrecovered mechanisms inside what looked like one
dispatch table: a second, independent kind-dispatcher (`0079DC`, 19+
kinds of its own), a cross-cutting shared tracker called from two
different entry points on real, witnessed paths (not a decline-and-move-
on arm), and a fifteen-way table whose handlers vary from trivial (`rts`)
to genuinely complex (`00772E`'s 10 real classes in 12 occurrences,
un-disassembled). The achievement tracker's own callee (`001648`) writes
through what looks like a hardware port or DMA target, which is a
different kind of fact than every leaf this session has modelled so far
(RAM reads/writes only) and needs its own investigation before any of
`004800`/`00475E`/the action table can be called a leaf, let alone
composed. This is System-scale work -- comparable to or larger than the
whole pickup-check subsystem this session already completed -- not a
bounded dispatcher family that fits in the remaining budget.

## Split (supervisor, 16 September, later the same day)

Read against the ROM and the retained fixtures, the three structures are
three ordinary shapes, none a new subsystem:

1. `0079DC` is a string copy — `move.b (a2)+,d1; beq; move.b d1,(a1)+;
   bra` until the NUL — of the record's message into the text buffer; the
   "19 kinds" were the copied strings' lengths, one census class per
   length.  A RAM-only leaf with a loop bounded by the NUL in ROM (cost a
   formula in the length, verified on the witnessed lengths); `007986`,
   its gate, a leaf beside it.
2. `001648` draws a collected item's icon into one of four VRAM slots: the
   VDP control command from the table at `0016C2` (`5E000003`…, indexed by
   D0) goes to `$4(a6)` with `a6 = C00000` on every witnessed call, then the
   descriptor's tile bytes (the same table `066794` the solids use, the
   descriptor chosen by D2 through `0019D2`, with two remaps of `6E`/`6D`)
   are streamed through the palette-remap table `0B5D04`/`0B5E04` (D1
   selects) to the data port; a negative D2 fills the slot with zeros or
   `DDDDDDDD`.  That is the sprite emitter's shape: a platform tail from
   the first device access to the RTS (recipe 6b), with a three-instruction
   prefix — worth owning only through its callers, `004790`/`0047DA` (the
   achievement-slot helpers) and from them `00475E`/`004800`, which then
   compose as seams the way `0018C8` does.
3. The action table at `0046D0`: twelve distinct handlers, nine witnessed
   (`004A0A`, `004D04`, `004E1C`, `004E74`, `0048EA`, `0048E4`, `004ACA`,
   `005024`, `00772E`), each censused as its own entry; classify each by
   shape (leaf, caller of recovered code, seam, dispatcher) and recover the
   admissible ones; the evaluator's firing arm then becomes a 6a
   dispatcher family over the record's `+10` word once its callees are
   owned.

Order for the grinder: (1) then (2) then (3); the firing arm last, as the
composition that removes the evaluator's declines.

## Resolution, part 1 (grinder, 17 September)

`0079DC` and its gate `007986` are recovered, each its own candidate
(`message-gate`, `string-copy`; `game/messages.py`).  `0079DC` is exactly
the plain byte copy the Split predicted: `move.b (a2)+,d1; beq; move.b
d1,(a1)+; bra` until the NUL, which is read but not written (the caller's
own `clr.b (a1)+`, right after the `bsr`, supplies the terminator) --
bounded by the string's own length, a formula in it, matching every one
of 39 retained fixtures across 19 distinct lengths (the "19 kinds" the
15 September census actually saw).

`007986` turned out to be a real shared priority gate, not a trivial
pass-through: it reads the display buffer at `MESSAGE_BUFFER` (`FFF166`)
and, if occupied, compares the caller's own priority (D7, negated first
when negative, which also clears `MESSAGE_BOUND`/`MESSAGE_PENDING`)
against the stored `MESSAGE_BOUND` (`FFF164`) -- a sufficient priority
still succeeds, through `MESSAGE_BUFFER_ALT` (`FFF16A`) instead, storing
the new bound either way.  The first census (2 recordings, `--classifier
kind`) saw only the empty-buffer arm; a fresh census over all eight
recordings (`--max-classes 100`, needed since the default 32-class cap
had already been shown this session to silently discard real classes)
turned up one real occurrence of the occupied-but-sufficient arm too --
both are now admitted (`'ready'`).  An occupied buffer with insufficient
priority (`'blocked'`, D0 -1) and one with a stored bound of exactly 0
(skips the compare outright, `'unrecovered'`) remain declined, unwitnessed
by any of the eight recordings.

Both gates: `factcheck check` MATCH on every retained fixture (20 for
`007986`, 39 for `0079DC`); `history-verify` PASS on `fb408bc75597…`
(`message-gate`: 31 hits, 0 fallbacks; `string-copy`: 69 hits, 9
fallbacks, all z80 bank guard); both mutants DIVERGENCE at frame 2,328;
tree of all eight recordings PASS, tree bit-exact (`artifacts/gods/
verify-camera-sprites-tree-2026-09-17g`).  Next: part 2 (`001648`'s own
icon-upload seam, through `004790`/`0047DA`/`00475E`/`004800`).

## Resolution, part 2, first bite (grinder, 18 September)

`0047DA` is recovered as its own candidate (`achievement-slot-reset`;
`game/achievements.py`), the innermost of the four callers this part
names, owned through the platform-tail shape (recipe 6b) directly: the
prefix is `0047DA`'s own RAM-only head (its `d1`/`d2`/`a0` frame, the
tracked-id slot store at `FFFFF22E`, deriving `D1` from whether `D0`
matches a caller-supplied "highlighted" id at `FFFFF236`) ending at the
`bsr` into `001648` itself; the ceded block is all of `001648`
(disassembled fresh: a VDP control command from the table at `0016C2`
indexed by `D0` into `$4(a6)`, then either a tile-descriptor stream
through the remap table `0B5D04`/`0B5E04` selected by `D1`, or -- the
Split's own open question (2) resolved: `a6` is the VDP data port, a
hardware register, exactly the device access it looked like, not a
software queue -- a fixed 8-longword fill of `FFFFFFFF` or `DDDDDDDD`
also selected by `D1`); the resume is `0047FA`; the suffix is `0047DA`'s
own frame restore and `rts`, RAM-only.  `0047DA` itself always sets `D2`
to `-1` before the call, so only `001648`'s own fill arm is reachable
from this caller (its real tile-descriptor upload belongs to whichever
other, unidentified caller reaches `001648` with a positive `D2` --
outside this part's own list).  `D0` (0-3) selects one of `001648`'s own
four VRAM icon slots through its table; 0, 1 and 3 are witnessed across
the eight recordings, 2 is real ROM code no recording ever calls `0047DA`
with: declined (`WITNESSED_ICON_SLOTS`).

`factcheck check` MATCH on all 9 retained fixtures (`census-0047DA-fresh-*`
