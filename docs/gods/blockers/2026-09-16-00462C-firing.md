## Where

  `triggers.evaluate_record`'s three conditions all hold). Census:
  `artifacts/gods/evidence/census-triggerfire` (node `fb408bc75597`,

  `007986`/`0079DC` (message), `004800`, `00475E`, and the fifteen action

## Code

`NEW_GODS_SUBSYSTEM`, in three independent places, not one dispatcher
family:

1. A message dispatcher inside `0079DC` itself, by a `kind` byte -- 19
   distinct kinds witnessed on one recording alone (`00`, `20`, `2C`,
   `31`, `37`, `5E`, `61`, `62`, `65`, `67`, `68`, `6C`, `6E`, `6F`, `70`,
   `72`, `74`, `77`, `79`), gated by `007986` (17 occurrences, 2 classes)
   returning non-negative first.
2. A collectible/achievement-slot tracker (`004790`/`0047DA`, reached
   from both `00475E` and `004800`) that, on a real match against one of
   four tracked ids (`FFFFF22E`/`F230`/`F232`/`F234`), calls `001648` --
   witnessed for real on 3 of 8 retained `004800` fixtures and 1 of 3
   retained `00475E` fixtures (not a theoretical branch). `001648` builds
   4-byte values from a byte-indexed lookup table (`0B5D04`/`0B5E04`,
   selected by a flag) sourced from the *same* solid/object definition
   table `0066794` that `00FDB8`/`00FC8E` (the solids subsystem) already
   reads, and repeatedly stores them to a *fixed, never-incremented*
   address `(a6)` -- a hardware port or DMA-style write, not a RAM
   buffer, and a real rendering effect this project's frame/PCM hashes
   would catch if modelled wrong. Earlier in the same routine,
   `move.l <address>,4(a6)` stores what looks like a *code address* into
   the same structure's own `+4` field -- the same "record with a stored
   continuation" shape the `0091BC`/`00FFF0` coroutine blocker
   (`docs/gods/blockers/2026-09-16-0091BC.md`) already flagged, here in a
   third, unrelated place.
3. The fifteen-entry action table at `0046D0` itself: three entries
   collapse to the same address `00475C` (a bare `rts`, witnessed 5,370
   times on this node alone -- essentially free), but ten of the other
   twelve distinct handlers were witnessed for real, with real variety:
   `00772E` alone shows 10 real path classes over only 12 occurrences
   (fixture `00772E-kind00-p0.state`, SHA-256
   `7199c384e1b296df11ed448c04f3e3e78cab28491dc3acf9dc3035f376c35fc8`, 78
   instructions, exits past its own entry at `007878`/`00787A` -- not yet
   disassembled). `004A74`, `004DBA` and `005074` were never witnessed on
   this node; the other nine (`004A0A`, `004D04`, `004E1C`, `004E74`,
   `0048EA`, `0048E4`, `004ACA`, `005024`, `00772E`) were, in varying
   depth.

## Observed

through the achievement-slot helpers `004790`/`0047DA`), `004800`-`0048E8`
(the second unconditional call, itself ending in the same achievement

"unconditional" calls (`0046BA bsr.w $4800`, `0046BE bsr.w $475e`) do not
show up under `00468A`'s own census entry at all -- `recovery_census`
single-steps a censused entry straight to its own caller return, so a
nested `bsr` reached only from inside an already-censused region is
folded into that region's own `calls` list rather than being counted as a
fresh top-level hit; they had to be re-censused as their own top-level
entries (`census-triggerfire2`) to get real occurrence counts and
fixtures at all. This is worth recording for whoever picks this up: any
further "callee of an already-censused entry" needs the same treatment.

unwitnessed arm.

## Why the current mechanism does not fit

This is not the single bounded "kind selects one of N self-contained
bodies" shape recipe 6a (and `00470C`'s own recovery) covers. It nests
three different unrecovered mechanisms inside what looked like one
dispatch table: a second, independent kind-dispatcher (`0079DC`, 19+
kinds of its own), a cross-cutting shared tracker called from two
different entry points on real, witnessed paths (not a decline-and-move-
on arm), and a fifteen-way table whose handlers vary from trivial (`rts`)
to genuinely complex (`00772E`'s 10 real classes in 12 occurrences,
un-disassembled). The achievement tracker's own callee (`001648`) writes
through what looks like a hardware port or DMA target, which is a
different kind of fact than every leaf this session has modelled so far
(RAM reads/writes only) and needs its own investigation before any of
`004800`/`00475E`/the action table can be called a leaf, let alone
composed. This is System-scale work -- comparable to or larger than the
whole pickup-check subsystem this session already completed -- not a
bounded dispatcher family that fits in the remaining budget.
