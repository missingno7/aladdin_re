## Code

## Observed

### `00A578`: not just a walk

The task's own one-line description ("walks the 9-slot creature list,
per creature calls `00A772`") undersells the real shape.  The outer loop
(`00A596`-`00A65C`, nine iterations via a pushed counter) reads each
list slot's own header word `(a3)`; zero skips the slot.  A **nonzero**
header selects one of two entirely different bodies, by whether the
header's own value is exactly `1`:

- **Header `== 1`** (`00A66A`-`00A76E`): the *per-frame update* body --
  loop over `d7+1` creature instances (`d7` from the type template's own
  `(a4)` byte), and for each one whose own lifecycle word `$8(a5)` is
  live, `bsr $a772` (the family, below).  Also handles icon-spawn
  transitions on lifecycle change, through the **already-recovered**
  `010D7C` (`game/spawns.py`, this session's own state-19/18 work) and
  `001164` (already in `camera-sprites`).
- **Header `!= 1`** (`00A5AA`-`00A648`, decrementing the header itself at
  the end): a *spawn-init* body -- for each of `d7+1` NEW instances,
  copies position from the type template (`$12(a4)`/`$13(a4)`), an
  unconditional `bsr $aa38` (unrecovered), a mode flag from `$100(a1)`
  (a tile-map/game-mode global, not yet identified), a direction-table
  index at `$a(a5)` combining that flag with a bit of `$4(a4)`, and a
  **second, independent** frequency-timer computation (`$14(a4)` byte *
  `FREQUENCY_SCALE`, `timers.py`'s own constant, into `$6(a5)`) -- a
  DIFFERENT timer field than `009D6C`'s own attack countdown at `$e(a5)`.
  A conditional `bsr $b920` (unrecovered) runs when the direction flag is
  zero.

Six new, unrecovered call targets fall out of this one routine alone:
`00AA38`, `00B920`, and (from the per-frame body's own further tail,
`00A6C8`-`00A76E`) nothing else new -- that tail is fully bounded over
already-recovered code (`010D7C`, `001164`).

## Why the current mechanism does not fit

Recovering `00A772` needs (at minimum) `00AA50`, `00A922`, `00B944` and
`00A9F2` bounded first (all four are unconditionally or near-unconditionally
reached on every activation); recovering `00A578`'s own spawn-init body
needs `00AA38` and `00B920`; recovering even the two most-witnessed kind
handlers needs `00AF3C`/`00AF52`, and the two "ground"/"fall" pairs share
`00AA38`/`00AA50`/`00AD68` plus a ROM table (`00AE4C`) whose contents are
not yet read.  That is at least ten new, entirely uninvestigated ROM
routines behind a single `009D6C`-sized recovery effort each (census,
trace, semantics, boundary plan, tests, the full verification ladder) --
a scope change from "recover the family and the walk" to "recover a
second creature subsystem," properly escalated rather than attempted
piecemeal.
