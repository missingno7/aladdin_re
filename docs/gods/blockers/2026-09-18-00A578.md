## Code

`NEW_GODS_SUBSYSTEM`: a creature-type dispatch table (`00A538`, eight
4-byte ROM pointer/flags pairs) over a per-instance record array, plus a
9-slot creature-*list* array (`FF1496`) that groups instances by type and
carries its own spawn-wave timing -- an interpreter-and-dispatcher shape
distinct from anything already named in `game/`, per the escalation
condition.

## Observed

### `00A578`: not just a walk

The task's own one-line description ("walks the 9-slot creature list,
per creature calls `00A772`") undersells the real shape.  The outer loop
(`00A596`-`00A65C`, nine iterations via a pushed counter) reads each
list slot's own header word `(a3)`; zero skips the slot.  A **nonzero**
header selects one of two entirely different bodies, by whether the
header's own value is exactly `1`:

- **Header `== 1`** (`00A66A`-`00A76E`): the *per-frame update* body --
  loop over `d7+1` creature instances (`d7` from the type template's own
  `(a4)` byte), and for each one whose own lifecycle word `$8(a5)` is
  live, `bsr $a772` (the family, below).  Also handles icon-spawn
  transitions on lifecycle change, through the **already-recovered**
  `010D7C` (`game/spawns.py`, this session's own state-19/18 work) and
  `001164` (already in `camera-sprites`).
- **Header `!= 1`** (`00A5AA`-`00A648`, decrementing the header itself at
  the end): a *spawn-init* body -- for each of `d7+1` NEW instances,
  copies position from the type template (`$12(a4)`/`$13(a4)`), an
  unconditional `bsr $aa38` (unrecovered), a mode flag from `$100(a1)`
  (a tile-map/game-mode global, not yet identified), a direction-table
  index at `$a(a5)` combining that flag with a bit of `$4(a4)`, and a
  **second, independent** frequency-timer computation (`$14(a4)` byte *
  `FREQUENCY_SCALE`, `timers.py`'s own constant, into `$6(a5)`) -- a
  DIFFERENT timer field than `009D6C`'s own attack countdown at `$e(a5)`.
  A conditional `bsr $b920` (unrecovered) runs when the direction flag is
  zero.

Six new, unrecovered call targets fall out of this one routine alone:
`00AA38`, `00B920`, and (from the per-frame body's own further tail,
`00A6C8`-`00A76E`) nothing else new -- that tail is fully bounded over
already-recovered code (`010D7C`, `001164`).

### The eight kind handlers: real, not uniform

Representative traces (`pathfacts`) of the six witnessed handlers show:

- `00ACA0` and `00AD88`: a "ground contact" shape, calling shared helpers
  `00AA38` (a grid/position-to-tile-address computation, structurally
  similar to `game.grid.grid_cell_at` but not confirmed identical),
  `00AA50` (an animation/table-lookup tail -- the SAME address `00A772`'s
  own `$f38a.w` arm calls, so it is reached two different ways), `00AD68`
  (a small collision-adjacent leaf) and a ROM table at `00AE4C`.  Their
  simplest witnessed path is short, but a representative alternate branch
  runs 70+ instructions.
- `00AE6C` and `00AED4`: a distinct "vertical fall" shape, also through
  `00AA38`/`00AA50`, with their own ground/ceiling-flag tail at a
  DIFFERENT record offset (`0x100(a1)`) than `00ACA0`'s own (`-1(a1)`/
  `0x7F(a1)`) -- confirmed by direct comparison, not assumed from the
  shared callees.
- `00AA76` and `00AB50` (the two most frequently hit, 331-481
  occurrences each) call their own pair of unexamined routines,
  `00AF3C`/`00AF52` -- not traced this session.
- `00AA80`/`00AB5A`: genuinely unwitnessed; decline by name, matching
  the eight-way table's own two never-taken kind slots.

## Why the current mechanism does not fit

Recovering `00A772` needs (at minimum) `00AA50`, `00A922`, `00B944` and
`00A9F2` bounded first (all four are unconditionally or near-unconditionally
reached on every activation); recovering `00A578`'s own spawn-init body
needs `00AA38` and `00B920`; recovering even the two most-witnessed kind
handlers needs `00AF3C`/`00AF52`, and the two "ground"/"fall" pairs share
`00AA38`/`00AA50`/`00AD68` plus a ROM table (`00AE4C`) whose contents are
not yet read.  That is at least ten new, entirely uninvestigated ROM
routines behind a single `009D6C`-sized recovery effort each (census,
trace, semantics, boundary plan, tests, the full verification ladder) --
a scope change from "recover the family and the walk" to "recover a
second creature subsystem," properly escalated rather than attempted
piecemeal.

## Progress (18 September, later the same day)

`00AA50` answered its own question directly: censused over all five
recordings (`census_all.py --entry 00AA50`), it turned out to be the
SIMPLEST possible shape -- twelve instructions, RAM/ROM-read only, no
store, ONE unconditional path with no branch at all (`census_all.py`'s
own `paths 1` on every one of the four recordings that reach it; the
fifth, `ca2b703b6fd5`, never does).  Recovered as its own standalone
candidate, `creature-frame-offset` (`ledger.md`'s own entry, full ladder
PASS including a milestone tree -- bit-exact on the four exercising
recordings, honestly `NOT_EXERCISED` on the fifth).  Not the "one region,
two gates" shape `009D6C` turned out to be: `00AA50` is genuinely one
physical routine reached from two different call sites, not two ROM
addresses sharing a body.  A real cross-reference finding: the instance
record's own field at `+0xA` (already named `DIRECTION_INDEX` from
`attack_update`'s own Q0 arm) is the SAME field this routine and
`00A772`'s own dispatch read as the record's own general KIND selector --
aliased as `game.creatures.KIND`, nothing renamed.  `00A922` and `00B944`
remain open, in that order per the task's own list (`00B944` wraps the
already-recovered pickup check `00BA8E`); `00A578`'s own two callees
(`00AA38`, `00B920`), the kind handlers and their own six callees, and
`00A772`/`00A578` themselves are all still outside this session's own
scope.

`00A922` was censused (`census_all.py --entry 00A922`, all five
recordings): called exactly as often as `00A772` itself (10023/8422/
1042/311 on the four recordings that reach it, `ca2b703b6fd5` never), but
NOT a same-shape leaf like `00AA50` -- 26/15/19/1 real path classes on
those four recordings respectively.  A representative trace
(`census-00A922-fb408bc75597/00A922-entry-p0.state`) shows three early
declines (`FFFFF1E6 != 2`, `FFFFF260 == 0`, `$10(a5) >= 0`, all `beq`/
`bne`/`bpl` straight to the routine's own `rts`) then a BOUNDED loop --
`FFFFF260` clamped to at most `0x14` (20) before the `dbra` even starts,
so the count is ROM/RAM-named, not unbounded -- scanning a fixed work-RAM
table at `FFFF0BF8` in 6-byte strides, reading a per-type word (`$4(a4)`)
shifted by a per-slot field (`$4(a0)`, compared against `0xC0`) and
masked to 2 bits, continuing (never a store, on this one trace) while
that value's own bottom two bits are zero.  Genuinely more work than
`00AA50` -- comparable in scope to `009D6C`'s own dedicated session, not
attempted further here; the loop's own real exit shapes (a nonzero
2-bit value, the `blt.b` arm actually taken, the `dbra` falling through
at `d7 == -1`) need `branches --vary` and the full retained-fixture set
before any semantics are written.
